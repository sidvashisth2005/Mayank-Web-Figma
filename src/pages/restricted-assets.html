Listing rules

Value is not enough.

Mayank is for assets that can move responsibly.

✓

Accepted

Ownership can be evidenced and the asset can move.

Products, code, domains, design systems, templates.
?

Conditional

The transfer depends on a provider’s permission.

App store listings, social pages, ad accounts.
✕

Declined

Refused, even when the work has commercial value.

Credentials, personal data, disputed work.

Never listed.

  1. 01
    Personal accounts or login credentials

    They belong to a person, and sharing them usually breaks the provider’s terms.

    Not accepted
  2. 02
    Customer databases or personal information

    The people in them never agreed to be sold.

    Not accepted
  3. 03
    Stolen, pirated or disputed work

    Ownership cannot be evidenced.

    Not accepted
  4. 04
    Non-transferable credits and subscriptions

    The provider does not allow them to move.

    Not accepted
  5. 05
    Accounts whose transfer is prohibited

    The seller has nothing they are allowed to hand over.

    Not accepted
  6. 06
    Manipulated metrics or illegal infrastructure

    Refused outright.

    Not accepted

Provider-dependent

Permission must be part of the route.

  • Provider permits itA documented transfer route exists.
  • Seller has authorityThey control the account and can show it.
  • Route is recordedSteps and timing are on the record.
  • Uncertainty is statedNever hidden from the buyer.
